04 / HOW IT WORKS

THE PIPELINE

NEXUS fuses computer vision with real-time 3D in a zero-dependency browser-native architecture. Built by Sarthak Mehta — no plugins needed.

01

Capture

MediaPipe accesses the webcam via WebRTC getUserMedia at up to 60fps.

02

Detect

TFLite palm detection locates hands; a 21-point landmark regressor maps each joint in 3D.

03

Mirror Correct

Since video is CSS-flipped for natural view, landmark X is corrected: mirroredX = 1 − rawX. Hand label is flipped too: MediaPipe's "Left" becomes Right in screen space, so we swap it.

04

Render

Gesture deltas drive Three.js mesh and camera transforms at 60fps via requestAnimationFrame.
