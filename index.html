04 / HOW IT WORKS

THE PIPELINE

NEXUS fuses computer vision with real-time 3D in a zero-dependency browser-native architecture. Built by Sarthak Mehta — no plugins needed.

01

Capture

MediaPipe accesses the webcam via WebRTC getUserMedia at up to 60fps.

02

Detect

TFLite palm detection + 21-point landmark regressor maps each hand joint in 3D space.

03

Mirror Correct

Video is CSS-flipped for natural view. Raw landmark X corrected: mirroredX = 1 − rawX.

04

Classify & Render

Gesture classifier runs on landmarks. Three.js updates 3D scene at 60fps via RAF.
